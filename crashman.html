CRASH MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Avoid the Walls: The Crash Bombers he shoots will stick to the walls or floor and detonate after a short delay. Avoid getting cornered and try to keep the fight in the center of the room.

  • Time the Air Shooter: Get relatively close to him, fire your weapon and will force him to jump and launch the tornadoes right into his jumping path. Repeat this and the fight is over in seconds.

  • The Bait-and-Switch: Run toward him, press the shoot button once to bait him into jumping and immediately slide under him as he leaps into the air. Then he will miss you entirely and shoot him when he lands.

  • Weakness: The AIR SHOOTER kills him in very few hits. It works because Crash Man often jumps right into the miniature tornadoes.
  • The Corner Trap: Walk to one side of the room and stand still. Wait for Crash Man to walk toward you. When he gets close, press the fire button. He will isntantly jump into the air and fire a bomb at the wall above you.

  • The Escape: As soon as he jumps, walk under him toward the center of the room. The bomb will stick to the wall and explode behind you.

  • Repeat: Turn around, walk back toward him, and do it again. By controlling when he jumps, he will never hit you.

  • Stage Tips: Halfway through the stage, you'll have to ride moving platforms attached to tracks. Don't jump prematurely.
