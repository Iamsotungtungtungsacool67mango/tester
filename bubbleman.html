BUBBLE MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Watch the Spikes: Because the chamber is flooded, your jumps carry you much higher than normal. If you hold the jump button too long, you will headbutt the spikes on the ceiling. Only tap the jump button lightly.

  • The Strat: When he is floating up and shooting, focus on dodging the bubbles and harpoon. As he glides down, run under him to switch sides. Once he lands, turn around and spam your weapon. If you are using the Metal Blade, just angle your shots upward and pelt him while he is floating.

  • Weakness: The METAL BLADE can fire Metal Blades diagonally or straight up, which lets you snipe him while he is floating around at the top of the screen.
  • Pattern: He starts by swimming upward toward the ceiling and firing 3 bubble projectiles and 1 harpoon at you. Once he reaches the top, he will glide downward toward the other side of the room. When he lands on the ground, he will pause breifly, then make a giant leap over to the opposite side of the arena.

  • Beat after Metal Man: His Metal Blade helps immensly with the fight.

  • Jump Strategy: Do not jump over the bubbles as they are very unpredictable with their floaty physics. Instead, weave between them on the ground. Only jump to clear the harpoon.
