FLASH MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Best Time to Attack: Right before he uses his Time Stopper. When he runs back and forth across the unever floor for about 5 seconds, it's your cue to jump.

  • Wall Juggling Trick: Stand completely still against a wall and let Flash Man run dieectly into you. Then, once he hits you, aim and fire a Metal Blade upward and will knock him in the air. If you keep firing the Metal Blade straight up, it will "juggle" him in a loop and traps him in the ceiling until he dies.

  • Mega Buster Method: the boss room has a bumpy floor, so use the raised platforms to jump over Flash Man when he charges at you.

  • Weakness: The METAL BLADE kills him in very few hits and can pelt him from across the room or when he tries to jump over you.
  • Flash Man's Blind Spot:Because of his terrible posture, his hitbox is a bit awkward. If you stay flat on the ground his walking animation will sometimes fail to register contact damage for a split second, giving you a tiny window to slip past him.

  • Beat after Metal Man: After you get the metal blade, the fight becomes extremely free.

  • Waste his Time Stopper: Flash Man must stop moving to cast the Time Stopper. If you hit him with a stagger-inducing weapon right as he pauses to freeze time, you can actually interrupt his animation. This forces him to reset his loop, meaning he will start running around again without ever getting to freeze you.
