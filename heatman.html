HEAT MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Best Time to Attack: Shoot him while he is walking, but be ready to jump when he stops to throw blades.

  • Mega Buster Strat: Stand on one side of the room and when he throws the 3 fire pillars, take small steps to let them crash safely beside you and then shoot him once. This will trigger his dash and then execute a jump over him.

  • Weakness: The BUBBLE LEAD get close and spam the Bubble Lead and takes 4 hits to kill.
  • Pattern: He throws three fire pillars at your current position and every time u hit him with a shot, he immediately closes his helmet and turns into a massive fireball that dashes toward you.

  • Defeat Bubble Man First: The Bubble Lead ends the fight in seconds.

  • Stage Tips: The hardest part about Heat Man's stage are the Disappearing Block Sections that appear and disappear in a strict rhythm. Listen to the rhythm and don't panic.
