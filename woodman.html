WOOD MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Best Time to Attack: The instant he lowers his shield is the best time to attack for Atomic Fire.

  • Corner Strategy: Stay in the far left corner when the fight begins. When Wood Man throws his Leaf Shield, wait for it to get close and perform a large jump over the shield. Then you will run and slide under him while he is mid air to switch sides. Turn around and blast him while his shield is down.

  • Weakness: A single fully charged ATOMIC FIRE shot wipes out nearly half of his health bar. Two shots will end the fight.
  • Pattern: Wood Man summons four leaves to surround him as a shield. At the same time, four more leaves float down from the ceiling in a zigzag pattern. He will launch his Leaf Shield straight at you and immediately after he will take a massive leap toward you.

  • Beat after Heat Man: Makes the fight incredibly easy with the Atomic Fire.

  • Stage Tips: The stage starts on the forest canopy, with two annoyances: Pipis who drop eggs that hatch into little enemies, and Battons that hang from the ceiling and drop down when you get close. Do not let the Pipi eggs hit the ground. Shoot the Pipi before it drops the egg. For the Battons, move forward slowly to trigger them, and step back and shoot them when they open their wings.
