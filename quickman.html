QUICK MAN

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Best Time to Attack: If you are using Flash Stopper or a fully charged Atomic Fire, the best time to attick is the second the battle begins.

  • Jump over the Dashes: When he charges horizontally, time a high jump to let him pass right underneath you.

  • Stay in the Center: The center of the room gives you the most room to slide under him or jump over him.

  • Weakness: The FLASH STOPPER completely freezes time, drianing Quick Man's health bar automatically.
  • Pattern: He throws three boomerangs that curva back to him. He performs 1-3 fast dashes or leaps across the room. He will leap high into the air, trying to land on you.

  • Defeat after Flash Man: The Flash Stopper obtained from him makes the battle too easy.

  • Stage Tips: The Force Beams make Quick Man's stage on of the most stressful levels in the Mega Man franchise. The Flash Stopper completely stops the lasers from firing.
