AIR MAN

Metal Man Quick Man Bubble Man Wood Man Air Man Heat Man Crash Man Flash Man

Tips to Beat the Boss

  • Best Time to Attack: The best time to deal massive damage is in the first 3 seconds of the match. Before he starts blowing wind, hold Forward on your D-pad to sprint right up to him and mash the attack button.

  • Do Not Jump During His Tornado Waves: Air Man's wind propeller continuously pushes you backward. If you jump while he is blowing wind, it will carry you backward into the trailing edges of the tornadoes.

  • Weakness: The LEAF SHIELD clogs his propeller and tears him to pieces.
  • Pattern: He stands on one side of the screen and shoots 3 separate waves of tornadoes, using his wind tunnel to push you back. Then, he will pause and take two large jumps to the opposite side of the arena.

  • Highly Recommended Early Game: He drops the Jet, which is a horizontal rocket-sled platform that lets you fly across massive gaps and can help you skip lasers in Quick Man's stage and the trick platforms in Heat Man's stage.

  • Jump Strategy: After Air Man fires his third wave of tornadoes, he stops blowing wind and prepares to leap across the room to switch sides. This is you ruce to take a big jump to cross paths in mid-air and landing in the corner he just left.
